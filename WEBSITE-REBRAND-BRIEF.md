# Students Organize for Syria — Website Rebrand Brief

## Strategic shift

The website must reposition SOS from **students responding to a crisis** to **students shaping the future of Syria**.

SOS remains a student movement first. Leadership development, partnerships, training, mentorship, and professional pathways strengthen the movement; they do not replace it as the organization’s identity.

## Core message

> Students are not waiting for Syria’s future. We are building it.

SOS builds the next generation of Syrian advocates, organizers, leaders, and changemakers by growing community power across campuses and communities.

## Brand principles

- Student-led and youth-owned
- Bold, unapologetic, and hopeful
- Grassroots energy with professional execution
- Community power over institutional polish
- Action-oriented rather than crisis-oriented
- Syrian voices and student organizers at the center

## Visual system

### Typography

- Large, tightly spaced editorial headlines
- Bold sans-serif typography
- Inter as the primary typeface
- Strong contrast between oversized statements and compact uppercase labels

### Color palette

- Deep charcoal: `#1B1C1A`
- Off-white: `#F4F0E6`
- Flag-inspired green: `#16824B`
- Warm cream: `#E8DCC7`
- Muted red: `#A63A32`
- Acid green highlight: `#B9E356`

### Image direction

The people are the brand. Prioritize student organizers, chapter meetings, conferences, workshops, leadership summits, speakers, networking events, and campus actions.

Photography should carry motion and feeling through blur, color vibration, saturation, grain, cropped compositions, and layered print textures. Avoid generic humanitarian imagery or images that reduce Syria to crisis alone.

## Homepage structure

1. Movement-first hero and immediate chapter action
2. Strategic manifesto explaining the organization’s evolution
3. Current organizing priorities and campaigns
4. Documented accomplishments and partnerships
5. Animated national chapter network
6. People-centered movement statement
7. Direct start-a-chapter and membership actions

## Chapter experience

An animated two-row college rail communicates the scale and energy of the network. It pauses on hover and each college card directs visitors to the existing chapter directory. Individual chapter URLs should replace the temporary directory links when SOS supplies them.

## Content safeguards

- Do not publish unverified chapter counts, member totals, or impact figures.
- Verify whether historical chapters are currently active before labeling them active.
- Replace legacy application, membership, and donation URLs once current destinations are confirmed.
- Confirm usage rights before publishing Pinterest-sourced reference artwork or third-party photography.

## Reference sites

- March For Our Lives: accomplishments and movement credibility
- Sunrise Movement: campaigns and organizing priorities
- Net Impact: long-term chapter network structure and scalable information architecture
